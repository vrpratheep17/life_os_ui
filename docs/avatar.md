# Dashboard avatar

The dashboard uses the supplied `public/models/Ch38_nonPBR.fbx`, refined into `public/models/ch38-avatar-v2.glb`. The original FBX and first converted GLB are preserved. The upgraded derivative keeps the character's appearance, textures, clothing, and skeleton, with a relaxed arm and finger pose and adjusted material roughness.

`scripts/avatar/motions.mjs` authors Idle, Wave, Nod, and LookAround clips at 60 samples per second. Gestures use eased timing, coordinated head/torso motion, and finger opening during the wave. Idle shifts the hips and bends the knees, with coordinated shoulder/arm/forearm motion and small finger adjustments; wrists follow the arms. `scripts/avatar/standing-balance.mjs` uses two-bone IK to preserve foot positions and orientations during the weight shift. Hands hang closer to the body with a light, varied finger curl. These are procedural clips, not motion capture or a newly sculpted character.

`scripts/avatar/blink.mjs` adds a model-specific Blink shape to the face and eyelashes and a 220ms Blink clip. The viewer plays it every 3–7 seconds while motion is enabled, independently of gestures, and opens the eyes when motion is paused. `node scripts/avatar/preview-face.mjs` and the `--closed` option render local face previews into `tmp/` for checking the eyelid shape.

The wave solves the shoulder, elbow, and palm orientations using the skeleton's actual hand landmarks. The elbow stays below the shoulder at roughly 70 degrees, fingers point up, and the palm faces forward. A small wrist sweep and secondary forearm motion replace the earlier straight-arm swing; elbow bending and lowering are staggered. Validation checks these constraints throughout the held greeting.

`scripts/avatar/work.mjs` adds a six-second seated Work loop with grounded feet, arm IK over the keyboard, finger taps, and small breathing/head motion. `DeskScene.tsx` provides the matching table, chair, and laptop in metres. The Work at desk / Stand up switch changes the base loop and camera view. Standing gestures remain available after standing up; blink and pause work in both modes. Validation checks desk height, hand reach, and grounded seated feet.

`scripts/avatar/sleep.mjs` adds a six-second Sleep loop with a horizontal pose, hands resting near the abdomen, gentle torso breathing, and closed Blink shapes. `BedScene.tsx` supplies the bed, pillow, and duvet. Sleep in bed / Wake up switches modes and frames the bed; automatic blinking is disabled during sleep so the eyes remain closed. Standing and desk modes are preserved. Validation checks horizontal alignment, pillow height, and closed eyes throughout the loop.

Run `npm run avatar:import` to regenerate the asset, then `npm run avatar:validate` to check textures, scale, all four clips, angular motion limits, planted feet, and returns to rest. Textures are limited to 1024px for mobile loading. Seven glTF warnings describe the shared normalization parent for the skinned meshes; there are no validation errors. Conversion reports unsupported FBX shininess-exponent data; color and normal maps are preserved in standard materials.

The earlier procedural model remains available separately:

Original procedural character generated by `scripts/generate-avatar.mjs`.

- `public/models/life-os-avatar.glb`: materials, 17-bone skeleton, Idle and Wave animations.
- `public/models/life-os-avatar.obj`: static geometry only; use GLB for animation and materials.
- `public/models/life-os-avatar.json`: joint names and clip metadata.

Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-avatar.mjs`; validate with `node scripts/validate-avatar.mjs`.

Edit geometry, colors, joint positions, and animation tracks in the generator. Import the GLB into Blender for further modeling and animation. The character is an original simplified model, not an exact reconstruction of the earlier generated image. Its limbs use rigid skin weights for a rounded toy-like style; it has no facial blend shapes or finger rig.

The viewer at `/dashboard` supports orbit, bounded zoom, wave, nod, look around, pause/resume idle, reset view, and model downloads. Gestures fade in/out; reduced-motion preferences disable automatic idle while allowing explicit gestures. A message and download links remain available if rendering fails. The 3D viewer is loaded separately from the landing page.
